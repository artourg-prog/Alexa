Sofia uses Vosk speech recognition (Apache License 2.0).
https://github.com/alphacep/vosk-api

Bundled model archives:
vosk-model-small-en-us-0.15
vosk-model-small-ru-0.22
Both are listed as Apache 2.0 by the official model catalogue:
https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models

Original model authors: Alpha Cephei / Vosk contributors.
Exact archive hashes and download sources are in scripts/fetch-voice-models.py.
The original archives are bundled unchanged. They are unpacked into private storage.

The multilingual backup uses whisper.cpp v1.9.5 (MIT license) and
Whisper Base quantized Q5_1 model weights, distributed through the official
https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp and
https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp repositories.
See WHISPER-LICENSE.txt. Source/model hashes: scripts/fetch-whisper.py.
Audio stays in a bounded on-device memory buffer and is not uploaded.
