# Sofia Clock 0.1 — separate installed app

The owner requested a standalone app after the integrated clock in counter 0.45 did not match the intended separation. Package `com.tailorshop.sofiaclock`, launcher label Sofia Clock, code1/version0.1. Reference: owner photo of a horizontal desk display, then explicit request for large София at top, nearly edge-to-edge time, date below, Las Vegas weather, black background and dim grey digits. Battery percentage is small. No speech models, microphone/camera/location permissions, private shop configuration or business endpoints.

Built and separately installed over USB on the authorized Fold. The counter app remains installed as 0.45. The standalone clock process and its own rendered view were verified; actual device UI reported the current time, Las Vegas weather and battery. NWS weather refreshed on the phone. Landscape was observed as 2176×1812; app-level Android16 compatibility property is used, without changing global rotation. The integrated counter Daytime clock setting was turned off through its settings UI to prevent duplicate clock modes.

Policy: when the app is open on external power, hold screen from 10:45 until20:00 local Las Vegas time. On battery, in background or after hours, release the hold and allow system timeout. A deliberate launcher opening provides a five-minute after-hours preview. Brightness8% during day/preview,2% after; dim text on black; slight minute drift. No automatic morning launch or native Samsung AOD replacement. No guarantee of zero burn-in, heat or battery use. Tap opens Sofia; hold opens preview/open/exit menu. Existing lock/PIN and receipt/report/SMS/pickup data are untouched by the clock app.

Validation: 3 JVM tests passed (hours/power boundaries, NWS values/nulls/old-data labeling/cache, drift bounds), 1 emulator instrumented UI case passed (no microphone permission; dim brightness; screen hold released at night/off power; manual preview; face rendering). Render visually inspected. APK signer verified before install. Business APIs were not exercised by clock tests.
